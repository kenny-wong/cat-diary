Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

My Little Diary is a personal online diary. Your diary is private: only you can read it when you are logged in. This page explains what is stored and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, also your Google account ID. We receive only your name and email from Google, never your Google password.
  • Your diary: the pages you write (text, date, mood, weather, template and stickers) and the photos you add.
  • A login cookie: a random code that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

How it is used

Only to run your diary: to log you in and to show you your own pages on any device. Your diary is not read by us, not shared, not sold, and not used for advertising or to train any software.

Where it is kept

Everything is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this website and its database. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords, if you use one, are stored only as a secure hash, never as plain text.

Your choices

  • Use Export in the diary at any time to download a copy of all your pages and photos.
  • To delete your account and everything in it, email kaiyanwong@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. It will be removed within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy: kaiyanwong@gmail.com.
