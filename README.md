# My Little Diary 🐾

A cute, decorated online diary. Everyone gets their own private diary, and it follows you to any phone or computer.

**Open it at https://my-little-diary.pages.dev**

## Getting started

1. Open the link and tap **Continue with Google**.
2. Your diary is ready. Write today's page, and it saves by itself.
3. To change the name friends see, open the account menu (top right).

## Writing a page

- **Mood and weather**: pick them at the top of each page.
- Under each page you can see when it was **last edited** (on friends' pages too).
- **Sports and activities**: next to the weather, pick what you played and what you did that day (work, study, a tournament, church, the park, shopping, travel and more).
- **Templates**: dress the page in Cat Café, Sakura, Seaside, Forest, Starry Night or Vintage.
- **Stickers**: cats, washi tapes, labels and emoji. Drag to move, pull the corner dot to resize or spin, and tap × to remove.
- **Photos**: add from the sticker box, or drop or paste them onto the page. Choose a polaroid, taped, round or cut-out frame.
- **Drawing**: tap ✏️ **Draw** in the sticker box and doodle. **Done** turns it into a sticker.

## Finding old pages

- The **calendar** shows your mood on each day. There's also a year view and a Year › Month list.
- **Search** finds pages by their words, and highlights them on the page you open.
- **Export** in the account menu saves a backup, photos included, and **Import** brings it back.

## Friends

- Tap 👫 **Friends** and type a friend's Google email. They need to have opened the diary once already.
- They get a request they can accept or decline.
- Each page has a 🔒 **Private** / 👫 **Friends** switch. Friends only ever see pages switched to Friends. In the account menu you can choose which one new pages start with.
- **Read a friend's diary**: open their calendar from the Friends panel.
- **Best friends**: tap ⭐ on up to 4 friends. Their page for the same day shows next to yours, with tabs if you have more than one.
- The Friends panel shows who is **online now**, when the others were last active, and when each one last wrote.

## Comments and notifications

- **Comment, reply and react with emoji** on shared pages. New comments appear by themselves, like a chat.
- **Mention a friend**: type **@** in your page or in a comment and pick a friend from the list (or type their first name, like @Chloe). They get a notification. On your own page, friends are told once the page is shared with friends, and the line under the page shows who's tagged.
- The 🔔 **bell** shows new friend requests, mentions, comments, replies and reactions.
- **Phone and computer notifications**: in the 🔔 panel, tap **Turn on notifications on this device**. They arrive even when the diary is closed. On iPhone or iPad, first use **Add to Home Screen**.

## Privacy

- Sign-in is through Google only, so the diary never stores a password.
- Your pages are private unless you switch them to Friends.
- Details are in the [privacy policy](https://my-little-diary.pages.dev/privacy) and [terms](https://my-little-diary.pages.dev/terms).

## Running it yourself

```sh
npm install
npm run db:migrate:local
npm run dev      # http://localhost:8790
npm test
npm run deploy
```
